# Dynamic-Dimensional-Reduction

A machine-checked **Lean 4** formalization of the paper

> Ahmed Bou-Rabee,
> [*Dynamic dimensional reduction in the Abelian sandpile*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.05968),
> Communications in Mathematical Physics 390 (2022), 933-958; arXiv:2009.05968.

Every theorem, lemma, proposition and corollary of the paper is formalized and proved.  The
development is built on [`mathlib`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4) and the
shared library [`Lattice-Probability`](https://github.com/nitromannitol/Lattice-Probability)
(`LatticeProb`).

[![CI](https://github.com/nitromannitol/Dynamic-Dimensional-Reduction/actions/workflows/build.yml/badge.svg)](https://github.com/nitromannitol/Dynamic-Dimensional-Reduction/actions/workflows/build.yml)

## What is proved

Start with `2d` chips at every site of the hypercube `C_N^{(d)} ⊆ ℤ^d` and topple in
parallel: at each step every site holding at least `2d` chips sends one chip to each
neighbour, and chips that leave the cube are lost.  The odometer `v_t^{(d,N)}(x)` counts the
topplings at `x` by time `t`.  The main theorem of the paper (Theorem 1.1) says that, on the
sorted fundamental domain `S_M^{(d)} = {M ≥ x_1 ≥ ⋯ ≥ x_d ≥ 1}` with `M = ⌈N/2⌉`,

    v_t^{(d,N)}(x_{d-1}, 1) = v_t^{(d-1,N)}(x_{d-1})      for every t ≥ 1,

so the centre slice of the `d`-dimensional sandpile runs the `(d-1)`-dimensional one for all
time (dimensional reduction), that the terminal odometers agree one layer further in, and
that the odometers of cubes of different sizes agree near their corners up to a topple time
`τ_j` (self-similarity).  Corollary 1.1 transfers both statements to the sandpile itself.
The proof goes through a discrete parabolic regularity of the odometer: Section 2 proves a
least action principle, symmetry and derivative comparisons, Section 3 computes the
odometer explicitly on cubes of side 2 and 4, and Sections 4 and 5 prove Proposition 4.1
(five regularity clauses) and Theorem 1.1 together, by one induction over dimension, side
length and time.

**The formalization is complete.**  Every theorem, lemma, proposition and corollary of the
paper is registered as a frozen Lean statement
(`python3 -m leanform_tools.check_coverage`), 20 registered statements in all, and **all 20
are proved**: each is `SEALED` in `ledger/manifest.yaml`, with a complete proof whose axiom
closure is exactly `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`.  The build emits no
warning and no `sorry`.  Lemmas 2.1 to 2.5, Corollaries 2.1 and 2.2, Proposition 3.1,
Lemma 3.1 and Proposition 3.2 are the results of Sections 2 and 3.  The other ten, the three
clauses of Theorem 1.1, the two clauses of Corollary 1.1 and the five clauses of
Proposition 4.1, come from the joint induction of Sections 4 and 5, proved as
`DimRed.reg_proved` in [`DimRed/Support/RegProved.lean`](DimRed/Support/RegProved.lean).
Not formalized: the introduction's discussion, the open problems of Section 1 and the
figures.

**Nothing is assumed.**  No result the paper cites is taken as a hypothesis;
[`ASSUMPTIONS.md`](ASSUMPTIONS.md) records this.  The correspondence between the paper and
the Lean statements, with the vocabulary, the standing hypotheses the frozen statements write
out and the junk values, is in [`CORRESPONDENCE.md`](CORRESPONDENCE.md).

### Main results

All of the paper's results are proved, the main ones by the joint induction of Sections 4
and 5.  [`DimRed/MainTheorems.lean`](DimRed/MainTheorems.lean) states every one of them in
full, each by `exact` of its certified counterpart in `DimRed/Frozen/`, so the statements
displayed there are the certified ones.  None takes a cited result as a hypothesis.

* **`DimRed.dimensional_reduction`**, **`DimRed.terminal_dimensional_reduction`** and
  **`DimRed.self_similarity`** (Theorem 1.1): dimensional reduction on the centre slice at
  every time, dimensional reduction of the terminal odometer one layer further in, and
  self-similarity across cube sizes up to the topple time.
* **`DimRed.sandpile_dimensional_reduction`** and **`DimRed.sandpile_self_similarity`**
  (Corollary 1.1): the same two statements for the terminal sandpile.
* **`DimRed.base_dimension_consistency`**, **`DimRed.base_dimension_weak_compatibility`**,
  **`DimRed.base_dimension_strong_derivative`**,
  **`DimRed.base_dimension_strong_compatibility`** and
  **`DimRed.base_dimension_strong_topple`** (the five clauses of Proposition 4.1).
  The joint induction proves these together with dimensional reduction and self-similarity:
  `DimRed.reg_proved` (`DimRed/Support/RegProved.lean`) applies `reg_all`
  (`RegAll.lean`) to the three step theorems `consist_step_of_earlier`
  (`ConsistStepFinal.lean`), `facet_step_of_earlier` (`FacetStepFinal.lean`) and
  `topple_step_of_earlier` (`ToppleStepFinal.lean`), and the frozen statements are read off
  by the bridges in `FrozenFromReg.lean`, `FrozenFromRegOdd.lean` and
  `FrozenFromRegMain.lean`.  `FrozenFromRegOdd.lean` covers odd cubes `N = 2M - 1` and the
  unit half-side `M = 1`, which the paper treats only in passing.
* **`DimRed.pt_ind_lemma`** (Lemma 2.1): the parallel toppling recursion
  `v_{t+1}(x) = ⌊(s_0(x) + ∑_{y ∼ x} v_t(y)) / 2d⌋` for `0 ≤ s_0 ≤ 2(2d) - 1`.
* **`DimRed.parabolic_comparison`** (Lemma 2.2): the parabolic least action principle.
* **`DimRed.symmetry_lemma`** (Lemma 2.3): the odometer of a symmetric pile is invariant
  under the symmetries of the hypercube.
* **`DimRed.general_difference_comparison`** (Lemma 2.4): the derivative comparison.
* **`DimRed.axis_monotonicity`** (Corollary 2.1) and
  **`DimRed.derivative_bound`** (Corollary 2.2).
* **`DimRed.topple_limit`** (Lemma 2.5): weak topple control.
* **`DimRed.no_reduction_at_critical_dimension`** (Proposition 3.1): dimensional reduction
  fails on the cube of side 2.
* **`DimRed.radial_laplacian`** (Lemma 3.1) and **`DimRed.base_case`** (Proposition 3.2):
  the explicit odometer on the cube of side 4.

### Scope and faithfulness

Every theorem, lemma, proposition and corollary of the paper is registered as a Lean
declaration.  There are 20 registered statements: the 12 numbered results of Sections 1 to
3, with Theorem 1.1 split into its three displays and Corollary 1.1 into its two, and the
five clauses of Proposition 4.1.  The following words have fixed meanings.

| term | meaning |
|---|---|
| **registered statement**, also called a **node** | one Lean declaration transcribing one statement of the paper. Each has an entry in `ledger/manifest.yaml`. |
| **frozen statement** | the declaration's text between the lines `-- FROZEN-STATEMENT-BEGIN` and `-- FROZEN-STATEMENT-END`, pinned by the SHA-256 of those bytes. It cannot change without the change being recorded in the manifest. The proof after the end marker may be rewritten freely. |
| **`SEALED`** | the node is **proved** here: a complete proof, whose axiom closure contains only `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`. |
| **`DRAFT_SORRY`** | the node is **open**: its statement is frozen and its proof is a single registered `sorry`. No node is in this state. |
| **axiom closure** | the axioms a proof ultimately rests on, as reported by Lean's `#print axioms`. |
| **`sorryAx`** | the axiom Lean inserts for an unproved `sorry`; its presence in an axiom closure means the result is not proved. |

Every registered statement is frozen in the sense of being pinned by hash, and every one is
proved.  The pin means that each proof is of the statement registered here and not of a
weaker one.

The paper is `paper/dimred.tex`, the arXiv source (arXiv:2009.05968v2) with the corrections
listed in [`paper/CHANGES_FROM_ARXIV.md`](paper/CHANGES_FROM_ARXIV.md), and every frozen
statement states what that file states; the unmodified arXiv source is
`paper/dimred-arxiv.tex`.  The figures and the bibliography file are not included.
[`CORRESPONDENCE.md`](CORRESPONDENCE.md) records the vocabulary, the standing hypotheses the
frozen statements write out, and the junk values.

Checkers compare the Lean statements with the paper's rather than with their hashes.
`python3 -m leanform_tools.check_coverage` confirms that every theorem, lemma, proposition
and corollary of `paper/dimred.tex` is claimed by a node.  In each frozen statement whose
conclusion has an existential constant, `python3 -m leanform_tools.check_constants` checks
that it is bound before the paper's parameters.  `python3 -m leanform_tools.check_clauses`
and `python3 -m leanform_tools.check_exponents` compare assertion counts and exponents
against `ledger/readings.yaml`, which records a clause reading of each of the 20 nodes and
the 12 exponent waivers.  These are review aids; none proves equivalence between LaTeX and
Lean.

## Guarantees

- **No `sorry`** in the library.  Every registered statement has a complete proof, and
  `python3 -m leanform_tools.check_warnings` fails on any `sorry` or other warning.  The
  four comparator challenges under `Audit/` carry ten intentional statement-level `sorry`s,
  one per challenge theorem, which the corresponding solution files prove.
- **No custom `axiom`.**  Every registered statement reduces to `mathlib`'s three standard
  foundational axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`, verified by
  [`DimRed/Meta/AxiomsAudit.lean`](DimRed/Meta/AxiomsAudit.lean) for the results restated in
  [`DimRed/MainTheorems.lean`](DimRed/MainTheorems.lean) and by
  `python3 -m leanform_tools.check_axioms` for every registered statement;
  [`CERTIFICATE.md`](CERTIFICATE.md) records the axiom closures and the commands that
  reproduce them.
- **Independent check of the statements.**  Theorem 1.1, Corollary 1.1 and Proposition 4.1
  are restated, with every definition they use rebuilt from Mathlib primitives alone, in
  [`Audit/DimensionalReduction/Challenge.lean`](Audit/DimensionalReduction/Challenge.lean)
  (Theorem 1.1(i), both displays),
  [`Audit/SelfSimilarity/Challenge.lean`](Audit/SelfSimilarity/Challenge.lean)
  (Theorem 1.1(ii)),
  [`Audit/Sandpile/Challenge.lean`](Audit/Sandpile/Challenge.lean)
  (both clauses of Corollary 1.1) and
  [`Audit/Regularity/Challenge.lean`](Audit/Regularity/Challenge.lean)
  (the five clauses of Proposition 4.1).  Each challenge imports only Mathlib and states its
  theorems with one `sorry` each; the matching `Solution.lean` proves the same statements
  from the library, and
  [leanprover/comparator](https://github.com/leanprover/comparator) checks that the
  restatement is proved from the library.  All four pairs are accepted by the Lean kernel and
  by the independent nanoda kernel at the pinned tool revisions listed in
  [`Audit/README.md`](Audit/README.md).  The default build does not include `Audit/`, and
  the manual workflow [`comparator.yml`](.github/workflows/comparator.yml) runs the
  comparator on every pair.
- **Pinned toolchain.**  Lean `v4.32.0` ([`lean-toolchain`](lean-toolchain)), `mathlib` at
  revision `81a5d257c8e410db227a6665ed08f64fea08e997` and `Lattice-Probability` at
  revision `bbe0b90a7cf0517db0578139aaab7f152edbc45e`, with mathlib's own dependencies
  `batteries` (`023ce7d`), `aesop` (`a7dbf0c`), `Qq` (`38d591e`), `plausible` (`e12c191`),
  `ProofWidgets4` (`6e311e2`), `import-graph` (`7e9612b`), `LeanSearchClient` (`c5d5b8f`)
  and `lean4-cli` (`88679d0`) at the revisions recorded, in full, in
  [`lake-manifest.json`](lake-manifest.json).

## Size

About 20,000 lines of Lean in 102 modules (`DimRed.lean` and the files under `DimRed/`), of
which about 17,000 lines are code once comments and blank lines are removed, on top of
`mathlib` and the `Lattice-Probability` library.  The comparator surface under `Audit/` is
not counted.

## Building

The project uses [`elan`](https://github.com/leanprover/elan) (the Lean toolchain manager)
and Lake.  The toolchain is pinned in [`lean-toolchain`](lean-toolchain)
(`leanprover/lean4:v4.32.0`), so `elan` installs the right Lean version automatically, and
the dependencies are pinned to commits in `lakefile.lean` and
[`lake-manifest.json`](lake-manifest.json); `lake` fetches them, and there is nothing to
clone by hand.

```bash
lake exe cache get   # prebuilt Mathlib
lake build           # compile the library
lake build Audit     # the Mathlib-only comparator surface: challenges and solutions
```

`lake build` emits no warning and no `declaration uses 'sorry'` message.  The only `sorry`s
are the intentional ones in the challenge theorems under `Audit/`, which `lake build Audit`
reports.

```bash
lake build DimRed.Meta.AxiomsAudit   # print the axioms of the restated results
```

To run the comparator on one pair, build `leanprover/comparator`, `lean4export` (at the
toolchain's tag) and `landrun` at the revisions pinned in
[`.github/workflows/comparator.yml`](.github/workflows/comparator.yml), and run from the
repository root, for each `<X>` in `DimensionalReduction`, `SelfSimilarity`, `Sandpile` and
`Regularity`:

```bash
COMPARATOR_LANDRUN=<landrun> COMPARATOR_LEAN4EXPORT=<lean4export> \
  lake env <comparator>/.lake/build/bin/comparator Audit/<X>/comparator.json
```

and expect `Your solution is okay!`; [`Audit/README.md`](Audit/README.md) has the details,
including the standalone elaboration check of each challenge.

To use the library, `import DimRed` pulls in the whole development; the statements of every
result are in `import DimRed.MainTheorems`.

### Checking the development

The gates live in `tools/leanform/`, the shared `leanform_tools` package; they need Python
3.10 or newer and PyYAML (`python3 -m pip install -r tools/requirements.txt`).
`python3 tools/verify.py` runs every gate below and fails on the first failure; add
`--skip-lake` to run only the pure-Python gates, and continuous integration runs it on
request.  A single gate runs as
`cd tools/leanform && python3 -m leanform_tools.<gate> <repo-path>`.  The `check_axioms` and
`check_warnings` gates run Lean; the others read the manifest, the paper and the Lean
sources.

| gate (run as `python3 -m leanform_tools.<gate> <repo-path>` from `tools/leanform/`) | what it guarantees |
|---|---|
| `check_manifest` | every node's file holds exactly one frozen block whose SHA-256 equals `frozen_sha256` and which declares exactly the node's `export`; every file in `DimRed/Frozen/` belongs to one node; `DimRed/` contains no `axiom`, `admit` or `sorryAx`, and `sorry` only once in the proof of each open node (there are none) |
| `check_axioms` | runs `#print axioms` on every manifest export and lists each closure; it fails if a sealed node's closure contains `sorryAx`, or a draft node's closure does not |
| `check_warnings` | `lake build DimRed` emits one `sorry` warning per open node and no other warning (so no warning at all) |
| `check_constants` | in a frozen statement whose conclusion has an existential constant, the constant is bound before the paper's parameters |
| `check_coverage` | every theorem, lemma, proposition and corollary of `paper/dimred.tex` is named in the `source` of some manifest node |
| `check_clauses` | compares the assertion count of each paper statement with the conjunct count of its Lean statement, against the reading recorded in `ledger/readings.yaml` |
| `check_exponents` | compares the exponents written in a paper statement with those in its Lean statement, against the waivers recorded in `ledger/readings.yaml` |

Also in `tools/leanform/`: `python3 -m leanform_tools.paper_anchors <repo-path>` and
`python3 -m leanform_tools.paper_citations <repo-path>` check the `dimred.tex:<lines>` ranges
in the manifest and in the Lean docstrings against the paper's labels;
`python3 -m leanform_tools.sync_docs <repo-path>` checks (`--write` regenerates) the status
block of this file and the frozen-surface table of `CORRESPONDENCE.md`;
`python3 -m leanform_tools.assumptions <repo-path>` regenerates `ASSUMPTIONS.md` (`--check`
verifies it is current); `python3 -m leanform_tools.freeze <repo-path>` registers a frozen
statement in the manifest.
