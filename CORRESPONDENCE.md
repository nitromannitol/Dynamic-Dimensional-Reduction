# Correspondence between the paper and the formalization

The Lean statements follow the corrected edition
[`paper/corrected-arxiv/dimred-corrected-arxiv.tex`](paper/corrected-arxiv/dimred-corrected-arxiv.tex)
of the paper, whose section "Corrections to the published version" lists every change from
arXiv v2. Each statement below is a file of `DimRed/Frozen/`; its text is fixed by its SHA-256
in [`ledger/manifest.yaml`](ledger/manifest.yaml), and `DimRed/MainTheorems.lean` restates it
unchanged. The table gives, for each statement, the lines and the label of the corrected
edition it states. Six of the 29 entries are the defining equations of the folded process, the
physical pile, the terminal pile and the maximum, stated as theorems so that their text is fixed
too; the other 23 are the 20 statement clauses of the published paper and the added Lemmas 4.1
and 5.1.

## Objects and carriers

| Source object | Lean representation | Domain and meaning |
|---|---|---|
| physical cube | `inCube d N` / `Corrected.CubeSite d N` | Coordinates from `1-N/2` through `(N+1)/2`; the site subtype is used for piles. |
| sorted fundamental domain | `inSimplex d M` / `Corrected.SimplexSite d M` | `M >= x_1 >= ... >= x_d >= 1`. |
| physical odometer | `odo d N s₀ t` | Literal threshold recursion, zero outside the cube. Uniform specialization is `odoUnif`. |
| physical pile | `Corrected.pile d N s₀ t` | `s₀ + lap(odo)` evaluated only on a physical cube site; `sandp_succ` proves the source mutual recursion. |
| terminal odometer | `odoInf` / `odoInfUnif` | Supremum in ENat; infinity is retained. Uniform positive-dimensional finiteness is proved. |
| terminal pile | `Corrected.terminalPile d N hd` | Positive dimension and physical cube carrier; eventual finite-time pile equality justifies every integer conversion. |
| cube-local symmetry | `Corrected.CubeSymmetric` | Only initial values inside the physical cube must be symmetric. |
| folded process | `Corrected.foldedOdo` | Independent threshold recursion using `foldedNbrSum` and the source zero-extended `foldedLift`. |
| directed folded sum | `Corrected.foldedNbrSum` | Both signs for each coordinate, including repeated representatives and exterior zero contributions. |
| boundary hitting time | `toppleTime d j` | First positive time some inner-boundary site reaches j; an empty hitting set gives infinity. |
| finite nonempty maximum | `Corrected.maximum d M hne` | Actual `Finset.sup'`; no empty-domain branch. |

Lemma 2.3 gives both invariance under the cube symmetries and the identification of the
odometer with the folded process, for every integer initial pile and both cube parities; the
even reflection sends `0` to `1` and the odd reflection sends `0` to `2`. Lemma 2.1 has the
truncation `max(0, ⌊…⌋)` for signed initial values. Corollary 2.1, Corollary 2.2 and Lemma 2.4
hold for every constant integer initial height. The corner bound in Corollary 2.2 is a
hypothesis of the paper's statement; no other hypothesis is added.

Theorem 1.1, Corollary 1.1 and Proposition 4.1 are proved by one induction over dimension, side
length and time. Corollary 1.1(i) and the strong topple control clause are stated over the
physical pile and the finite maximum above; they agree with the plain expressions `sandpInfUnif`
and `simplexMax` by `rfl` and by `DimRed.Corrected.maximum_eq_simplexMax`. The clause readings
used by the repository's checks are in [`ledger/readings.yaml`](ledger/readings.yaml).

## Statement hashes

The SHA-256 of a statement is taken over exactly the UTF-8 bytes between its
`FROZEN-STATEMENT-BEGIN` and `FROZEN-STATEMENT-END` markers, dropping one initial newline.
`python3 tools/verify.py` recomputes every hash.

The nonnegative floor formula of the published Lemma 2.1 is kept as `bounded_point_update` and
used inside proofs where the initial pile is nonnegative; it is not a hypothesis of the stated
Lemma 2.1.
